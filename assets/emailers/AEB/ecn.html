Egg Enthusiast Summer 2025 Update
As an Egg Enthusiast, your passion for eggs is clear! And your patients and clients may already love them too. While they probably associate eggs with protein, there are other nutritional benefits of eggs that can support them on their personal wellness journey.
We all want to feel our strongest every day. In a perfect world, we’d have all the time we need to work out, meditate, connect with friends — to be our best self. But the realities of life often get in the way.
The good news is, even taking one step a day in the right direction can make a difference. And a step that’s easy for nearly everyone to take towards their strongest self: simply reach for that carton of eggs.
Just two eggs give you complete protein to fuel your body and a collection of nutrients that feed your brain — that’s a whole lot of what you need to feel that much stronger.1,2 The best part? The Incredible Egg delivers dozens of easy and delicious ways to get these benefits every day.
So, crack open this newsletter to learn more about how you can help patients and clients learn easy ways to support a stronger body and mind.
 

Back to School Meal Solutions

Eating two eggs provides 12 grams of high-quality protein and 50-70% of daily choline requirement3,4 — exactly what growing kids (and busy parents) need to power through the day.5,6 Eggs contribute lutein and zeaxanthin to the diet – antioxidants that are also found in fruits and vegetables.7 Emerging evidence shows there may also be a connection between lutein and cognitive function.8 Eggs offer nutritional support from head to toe!
