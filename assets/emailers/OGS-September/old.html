Exploring a New Outlook in User Engagement with WebGL
To enhance user experience through immersive online engagement, the Omnicom Global Solutions (OGS) team recently explored WebGL, an interactive 3D system that enables stunning scenes, animations, and engaging content in browsers. Some of the use cases we are looking to explore include immersive product views, real-time customization, realistic simulations, and many more applicable across industries.
Our team recently utilized this emerging trend in two of the projects, and the results were phenomenal. Whether it's about providing immersive product experiences in e‑commerce or increasing user engagement in advertising, the potential for WebGL is vast.
