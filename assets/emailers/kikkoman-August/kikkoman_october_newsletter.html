At Bonnie’s in Brooklyn, Chef Calvin Eng reimagines Italian arancini with a Cantonese twist—using a rich, concentrated congee base loaded with ham and cheese, then fried to golden perfection in crispy Kikkoman® Panko. The result: crunchy on the outside, creamy and savory on the inside—an irresistible mashup of comfort food traditions.

This soulful fusion brings together the deep spice of Louisiana gumbo and the umami warmth of Japanese ramen, celebrating the shared roots of resilience and creativity in both traditions. At its center are scratch-made boudin meatballs—crafted from andouille sausage and rice—glazed with honey, pineapple, and Kikkoman® Teriyaki for a uniquely bold flavor. Finished with a Southern-inspired deviled egg in place of the classic ramen soy egg, this dish is a vibrant bowl where flavor, heritage, and soul beautifully collide.

Nathaniel Malone, Corporate Executive Chef at Creative Dining Services, gives a global twist to classic Thai noodles with this bold, smoky dish. Wide rice noodles and smoked brisket burnt ends are tossed in a savory-sweet jus made with Kikkoman® Gluten-Free Hoisin Sauce, Soy Sauce, and Rice Vinegar, then finished with garlic-fried collards, peanuts, and pickled onions. It’s down-home comfort food with an international edge.

At the University of North Texas, Executive Chef Matthew Ward is raising the bar on campus dining with food that’s bold, balanced, and built for sustainability. For nearly a decade, he’s led Residential Dining, helping UNT earn “Best College Food in Texas” three years running and the #4 spot nationally (niche.com). Explore his take on dining trends and try recipes for four boldly flavored student favorites.

James Beard Award–winning chef Ken Oringer brings global flair to every dish on his menus, and his Hoisin Arepas are no exception. Made with Kikkoman® Gluten-Free Hoisin Sauce blended right into the batter, these golden corn cakes deliver a subtle sweet-savory umami that pairs beautifully with ricotta, parmesan, and corn. The result: a gluten-free spin on a Latin American staple that’s as versatile as it is unforgettable.

At her SoulPhil popup, Chef Tiana Gee reimagines Filipino kinilaw through the lens of Southern soul food, creating a bold, modern fusion that’s all her own. Featuring Kikkoman® Gluten-Free Tamari, this dish delivers rich umami without the gluten, topped with pickled okra, sweet potato chips, avocado, and a drizzle of blackened spice oil. A guest favorite that proves creativity know no limits.
