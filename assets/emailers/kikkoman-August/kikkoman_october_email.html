For the second year in a row, we invited foodservice professionals to show us how Kikkoman® Soy Sauce inspires their menus and their culinary imagination by entering the INSPIRED BY KIKKOMAN Chef Competition. It’s a contest with a culinary prize like no other: the chance to win a trip to Japan!

From hundreds of initial submissions, our judges invited 50 contestants to proceed to a second round. And from among those 50, they selected 5 finalists. This year, we added a fan voting component, so foodservice professionals like you could help us narrow down the finalists to select a Grand Prize Winner, a Runner Up, and a Fan Favorite.

Our huge thanks to everyone who submitted entries and participated in the voting process! And congratulations to our winners!

INSPIRED BY SOY SAUCE

Kikkoman® Soy Sauce is one of the most versatile umami enhancers in any chef’s arsenal. In celebration of National Soy Sauce Day (October 1), here are three recipes from leading-edge chefs around the country that use our Soy Sauce in innovative ways.

Spray It with Soy Sauce: At ChoLon Modern Asian in Denver, Chef Lon Symensma’s take on Singaporean coconut jam toast features an aerated egg cloud custard garnished with fresh cracked white pepper and misted with soy sauce.

A Wafu Take on Steak: From Chef Robbie Felice (pastaRAMEN, New Jersey) this Wafu Italian steak features a sesame miso butter enhanced with Kikkoman® Soy Sauce.

A Little Sweet, A Little Savory, A Lot of Fun: Pastry Chef Gemma Matsuyama of Tsubaki in Los Angeles serves these grilled rice flour–tofu balls with an umami rich sweet soy glaze.
