Discover Deco Maki

In Japan, deco maki are a fun, creative twist on traditional rolled sushi. To make deco maki, ingredients are shaped in a sushi mat to create sushi rolls that reveal a cross-section image or design when they’re sliced. This month, we’re sharing two how-to videos that reveal the secrets of making deco maki flowers and, amazingly, a portrait of Kikona, Kikkoman’s global brand mascot!
