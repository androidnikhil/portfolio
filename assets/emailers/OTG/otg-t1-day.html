This is your final reminder before your scheduled email migration. Below are key steps to keep in mind tomorrow:
What To Do Tomorrow When You FIRST Log On:
Check your location and internet connection:
  • If you are in an OMC office on migration day, you MUST use a WIRED LAN connection.
  • If a wired connection in an OMC office is unavailable, please follow these steps:
    • Log into your laptop using your existing account (do not start the omc.com migration steps yet)
    • Connect to the “OMCGroup-Guest” Wireless Network by following the “Connecting to guest wireless” guide linked HERE.
    • Once registered and connected, follow the step-by-step instructions in Migration Guide
  • If you are at home or at a non-OMC office location, you can use the available wi-fi
Restart your computer:
  • Click on the Start menu, select the power icon and select Restart
Log in as “Other user” with your new email address:
  • At the login screen, click on “Other user”
  • Enter your new email address in the User name field. (e.g. your.name@omc.com)
After entering your new email, type in your existing password:
  • You should now be logged in with your new email address and ready to start the migration process
To complete your email migration, use the Mac or Windows Migration Guide you saved earlier and follow the step-by-step process.
What To Do Post-Migration:
Confirm
  • Confirm you have access to your mailbox, calendar and confirm shared drives are loading correctly
  • Check that your applications are working, and your mobile device setup is correct
Test
  • Send a test email to yourself and a colleague to verify delivery using your new @omc.com email address
Update
  • Update your email signature to reflect your new address
  • Update any saved logins on applications or third-party tools
Report
  • Report any issues to the Hyper-Care Bridge for day-of migration troubleshooting, or reach out to the Paige Service Desk.
If you have any questions, please don’t hesitate to reach out to the OMC.com Program team (omc.com-program@omc.com).
