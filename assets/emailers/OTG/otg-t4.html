Transitioning your email address to @omc.com
As part of our enterprise-wide transition to @omc.com, your company email address will be updated on [Date]. Please read through this email to understand what is changing, what you can expect next and where to go if you need help.
What You Need To Know:
Your email migration date is scheduled for [Date]
Your primary email address will change from [original email] to [new email].
What You Need To DO:
Check Your Email Address:
  • if the primary email address we have listed above is incorrect, you must let us know by emailing omc.com-program@omc.com by [cut-off date].
  • If your new email address includes a number, this indicates you share the same first and last name as another Omnicom employee, and our system automatically assigned a number to prevent conflicts. If you would like to request another variation – such as adding a middle initial – please let us know by emailing omc.com-program@omc.com by [cut-off date].
    • The only modifications being made are in cases where duplicate names exist and variations will be limited to your legal and formal name as listed in your employee profile.
    • If you’ve recently changed your name (e.g. due to marriage or legal name changes) but have not updated it with the company, please follow the existing process to submit a name change request with Human Resources.
Please Note:
  • Your primary email address is the one used to login to your computer.
  • Secondary accounts will not be migrated.
  • No changes will be possible after [cut-off date].
  • Your email migration happens overnight - Be ready to complete the required steps first thing in the morning of your scheduled migration date.
Things to Keep in Mind:
New Username:
Your username will update to your new @omc.com email address and will be used to log in to your computer and applications.
Same Password:
While your username will update to your new email address, your password will not change.
Agency Branding:
Agencies will maintain their brand identity from an IT perspective including display names and email signatures.
  • Your display name – how your name appears in inboxes and on platforms like Teams – will continue to include your agency's name in parenthesis alongside your name.
  • Your email signature – the personalized block of text with your contact information and agency's branding – will continue being used.
Common Applications:
Microsoft 365, Adobe, and Netskope will be updated for uninterrupted access with your new @omc.com username. Other applications might need to be updated.
Step-by-Step Migration Guide:
Review the MAC and Windows end user guide to see the step-by-step instructions you will need to follow in order to complete the migration on your scheduled date.
Uninterrupted Communication:
Post-migration, you will continue to receive emails sent to your old address until at least December 2026.
Support Resources:
Transition to @OMC.com SharePoint: Visit our dedicated SharePoint site for this initiative for the latest information and helpful resources to help you navigate the change, including:
  • Employee Toolkit: A quick reference guide with transition best practices, employee actions overview and internal / external checklists.
  • Employee FAQs: Addresses the most frequently asked questions employees have to date.
  • Client FAQs: To help answer any client questions.
  • Client Director Resources: Materials to support employees responsible for communicating the news about Omnicom’s transition to @omc.com to clients.
We will continue to keep you updated as your migration date approaches, providing more details and reminders for two weeks, one week, and one day before your scheduled migration. If you have any questions, please feel free to contact the OMC.com Program team (omc.com-program@omc.com) at any time.
Thank you for your collaboration on this important initiative.
